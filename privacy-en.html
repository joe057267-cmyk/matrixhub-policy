Legal · Privacy

MatrixHubx Privacy Policy

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Last updated: October 28, 2026 · Version 2.0

MatrixHubx (“we”, “us” or “our”) provides multi-platform content scheduling, approval, publishing and analytics services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and delete your information when you visit MatrixHubx, use the MatrixHubx application, or connect third-party social media accounts.

Notice for TikTok users: This product obtains and transmits data through TikTok Login Kit and TikTok Share Kit. How we collect, store, use and delete your TikTok user data (including OpenID, avatar, display name and similar data) is described in detail in Section 4.2. We do not publish content without your confirmation, and we do not sell TikTok data or use it for advertising profiles.

1. Scope and operator

This Policy applies to the MatrixHubx website, web application, mobile pages, APIs and related services (collectively, the “Service”), operated by the MatrixHubx team (“we”, “us” or “our”).

This Policy does not apply to third-party social platforms, payment providers or other services provided independently by third parties. Your information on those platforms is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review them before use.

If you use the Service on behalf of a company or other organization, you confirm that you are authorized to accept this Policy on its behalf.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process your information only for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining your account, verifying identity and keeping you signed in;
  • Publishing content through official platform APIs on your behalf after you approve it;
  • Reading data from accounts you authorize to generate publishing records and analytics;
  • Providing customer support and handling disputes and data requests;
  • Protecting the Service against unauthorized access, abuse and attacks;
  • Billing, invoicing and complying with tax and other legal obligations;
  • Improving product features and reliability using aggregated or anonymized data.

Where the EU or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies, our legal bases are performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests (securing and improving the Service), your consent (for optional cookies or optional data), and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

We do not sell your personal information and do not use it for cross-site behavioral advertising. We do not use your private content to train general-purpose public AI models without your consent; when you actively use an AI feature, we process the relevant content only to generate the requested result.

4. Platform authorization data

4.1 How authorization works

When you connect a social account, we use the platform’s official OAuth flow. You are redirected to the platform, where you choose which permissions to grant. We receive only the scopes you approve and never ask for or store your platform password. Tokens are encrypted at rest on our servers and protected by least-privilege access controls and audit logs.

4.3 Google and YouTube API Services

When you sign in with Google or connect a YouTube channel, we request only the data required for the features you enable:

  • Basic profile information (name and avatar) and email address;
  • Basic channel information, video metadata, publishing status and authorized analytics for channels you authorize.

We use this data only to verify your identity and link your account, upload videos to channels you authorize, display analytics dashboards, and handle support requests related to YouTube features. We do not sell, rent or trade Google or YouTube user data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with infrastructure providers bound by confidentiality and data processing agreements, or when required by law.

Google and YouTube data is transmitted over HTTPS/TLS and stored using industry-standard safeguards, with access limited to authorized personnel. Tokens are retained only as long as necessary to provide the Service. After you revoke authorization or terminate your account, we stop accessing the data and delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period.

You can revoke access at any time at Google Account third-party connections. Our use of Google API data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube API Services is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

4.4 Meta platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Threads)

When you connect a Meta platform, we obtain through Meta’s official OAuth the Pages, Instagram professional accounts or Threads profiles you authorize, together with the permissions needed for publishing. We call APIs only within the scopes you grant, to publish content and read publishing results and analytics.

We comply with the Meta Platform Terms and Developer Policies. We do not sell Meta user data, do not use it for ad targeting, and do not continue accessing it after you revoke authorization. You can remove MatrixHubx in your Meta account’s “Apps and Websites” settings, or contact us under Section 12 to request deletion. Where a platform provides a data deletion callback, we complete deletion as required upon notice.

4.6 How to revoke authorization

You can disconnect any platform at any time in MatrixHubx’s account management, or revoke access in that platform’s “authorized apps” settings. After revocation, we stop accessing data through that authorization and delete the corresponding tokens and associated data within 30 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use a small number of cookies and browser storage entries to operate the Service:

  • Essential: sign-in session, security checks and load balancing. The Service cannot function without them.
  • Preferences: language, theme and sidebar state, stored in localStorage and removable at any time.
  • Analytics: if enabled, used only in aggregate for traffic and error rates, not to identify individuals or track you across sites. You can opt out through browser settings or our notice.

We do not use advertising cookies and do not participate in cross-site behavioral advertising networks. You can clear or block cookies in your browser, though this may affect sign-in and some features.

6. Sharing, processing, transfer and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent or at your request, when sending a publishing request to the relevant platform;
  • With providers of cloud hosting, object storage, email, payment and logging infrastructure, solely to support the Service under confidentiality and data processing agreements;
  • To comply with law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights of users, the public or ourselves;
  • In a merger, acquisition, restructuring or insolvency, where the recipient remains bound by this Policy; if the purpose changes, we will seek your consent again.

We do not publicly disclose your personal information except with your consent or as required by law. All processors are required to apply appropriate safeguards and may not use the data for their own purposes.

7. Storage, security and retention

Your information is stored on cloud infrastructure used by us and our processors, which may be located outside your country or region. We protect it through encrypted transmission, access controls, audit logging and employee confidentiality obligations. No internet transmission is absolutely secure, and we continually review and improve our safeguards.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will assess the risk as required by applicable law and notify you and regulators where necessary.
